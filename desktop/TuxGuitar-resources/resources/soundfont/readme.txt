Magic Sound Font v2.0

This soundfont was contributed by Dennis Deutschmann in November 2015 (Commit 72cd023e94d6ee015aba23e77b9c9c828b7f039a).
Dennis has permitted the distribution of the soundfont together with TuxGuitar. Beyond that, no license terms are known.

Attempts to contact Dennis to determine more specific license terms have been unsuccessful. Therefore, this file is usually not included in the packages of common Linux distributions. In such case, other soundfonts are used instead.

Here you can find some background information on this matter:

- https://sourceforge.net/p/tuxguitar/support-requests/13/
- https://bugs.debian.org/819332
- https://lists.debian.org/debian-legal/2023/01/msg00004.html
